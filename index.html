How It Works

1) Join

Create an account and become a Participant. In this stage, the participation counter tracks account growth.

2) Build

Submit pain points, propose solutions, debate ideas, and collaborate in the member app.

3) Transition

Transition is considered only when milestone thresholds are met and the community votes to proceed.

Launch Milestone Checklist

  • Ratified verification method for unique human participation
  • Foundational governance framework published and vote-tested
  • Founding Assembly roles filled and documented
  • Public safety standards: peaceful, lawful, anti-harassment
  • Open audit of member counts and voting integrity tests
