# LiquidGov.US

**Modernizing American self-governance through liquid democracy.**

This repository contains the definition, design, and eventually the source code for [LiquidGov.US](https://liquidgov.us) — an organization advocating for liquid democracy systems that give every citizen a continuous, meaningful voice in the decisions that shape their communities and their country.

## Repository Contents

- **[DEFINITION.md](./DEFINITION.md)** — The foundational document defining the mission, message, principles, organizational structure, website architecture, and open questions for LiquidGov.US.

## What is Liquid Democracy?

Liquid democracy is a hybrid of direct and representative democracy. Citizens can vote directly on issues they care about, or delegate their vote to someone they trust — and reclaim that delegation at any time. It combines the accountability of direct participation with the practical wisdom of trusted delegation.
