Definitions

Participant: any person with an account.

Verified Patriot: a Participant who verifies identity and signs the transition pledge.

Phase Transition: a planned shift from growth mode to launch mode after Launch Milestone criteria are met and approved.

Participation Standards

  • Participation remains voluntary and reversible.
  • Platform conduct must stay peaceful, lawful, and non-coercive.
  • Anti-harassment standards apply in all channels.
  • Historical records preserve proposal authorship and timing.

Minor Safety and Guardian Visibility

  • Participants under 18 cannot become Verified Patriots without a linked parent or guardian account.
  • Minor accounts default to interacting with other minors only, unless parent/guardian permissions are explicitly granted.
  • Parent/guardian investigations of a child account are logged and disclosed to the child account via notification.

Transparency Features

  • Profile visit logging to discourage covert harassment.
  • Proposal archive preserving implemented and archived ideas.
  • Implemented governance actions include origin credits for contributing members.
