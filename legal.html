Standards and Definitions

Participant: any person with an account.

Verified Patriot: an account that completes identity verification when enabled.

Launch Milestone: the transition point from growth to formal governance activation.

Participation Standards

  • Participation is voluntary and reversible.
  • Conduct must remain peaceful, lawful, and non-coercive.
  • Harassment, exploitation, and threats are prohibited.
  • Records preserve proposal authorship and timing.

Safety and Child Protection Standards

  • Sexual exploitation of minors is prohibited with zero tolerance.
  • Minor accounts require linked parent/guardian oversight features.
  • Minor-to-adult interactions are restricted unless explicitly approved by a guardian account.
  • Guardian review actions are logged and disclosed to the child account.
  • Abusive behavior may result in immediate moderation or account removal.

Transparency Features

  • Profile visit logging supports participant visibility.
  • Contribution archives preserve implemented and archived ideas.
  • Implemented governance actions retain origin-credit references.
