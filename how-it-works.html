How NUSA Works

NUSA is a voluntary civic platform for transparent coordination, proposal review, and participant-driven decision-making.

What is liquid democracy?

Direct participation

Participants can vote directly on proposals and leadership decisions.

Delegated participation

Participants can delegate representation and change it whenever needed.

Continuous adjustment

Civic decisions are updated through ongoing participation, not only election cycles.

Why NUSA

  • Transparent records for public accountability.
  • Scope-based collaboration from neighborhood to national.
  • Problem-to-solution workflow with contributor credit.

What happens when I join?

  1. Create an account with username and password only.
  2. Complete the 4-step onboarding checklist in the Member App.
  3. Post your first proposal and start participating immediately.
